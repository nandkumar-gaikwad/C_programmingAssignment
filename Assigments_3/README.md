## Programs

### 1. Print Even Numbers
- **Function Name:** `PrintEven`
- **Description:** Prints the first `N` even numbers.
- **Input:** `7`
- **Output:** `2 4 6 8 10 12 14`

### 2. Display Factors of a Number
- **Function Name:** `DisplayFactor`
- **Description:** Displays the factors of a given number, excluding the number itself.
- **Input:** `24`
- **Output:** `1 2 3 4 6 8 12`

### 3. Display Even Factors
- **Function Name:** `DisplayEvenFactor`
- **Description:** Displays the even factors of a given number, excluding the number itself.
- **Input:** `36`
- **Output:** `2 6 12 18`

### 4. Convert Character Case
- **Function Name:** `DisplayConvert`
- **Description:** Converts an uppercase character to lowercase and a lowercase character to uppercase.
- **Input:** `A`
- **Output:** `a`

### 5. Check Vowel
- **Function Name:** `Chkvowel`
- **Description:** Checks whether a character is a vowel. Supports both uppercase and lowercase vowels.
- **Input:** `a`
- **Output:** `It is vowel`
